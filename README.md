# AI-POWERED-WHEELCHAIR
Eye-Glance Steering Put the ECG electrodes at your temples and the module reads eye movements (EOG). Glance left or right to steer a rover or small powered-chair prototype, and use a jaw clench (piezo at the temple) for stop/go. The module's built-in high-pass filter sees quick glances rather than sustained gaze, which suits event-based commands.
